## Standing Orders

Source: [Standing Orders](https://www.churchofengland.org/national-bodies/general-synod/synod-members-resources/standing-orders). The Standing Orders lay out the rules under which the General Synod regulates its proceedings. They cover general procedures at a group of sessions, motions and amendments, other procedures and customs, Measures and Canons, legislative reform orders, liturgical business, financial business, questions, elections, and the Crown Nominations Commission. The Standing Orders Committee reviews them regularly.

- [Standing Orders, updated July 2026](https://www.churchofengland.org/sites/default/files/2026-08/standing-order-updated-jul-26.pdf) (the latest version)

## General Synod app

Source: [General Synod app](https://www.churchofengland.org/national-bodies/general-synod/synod-members-resources/general-synod-app). The free app gives access to the agenda and papers for current and past Synod sessions, with an archive back to February 2023. Access is restricted to Synod members, National Church Institutions staff, and third parties accredited by the Synod support team. Accounts are created by the Synod Support team; the username is the email address registered with General Synod.

- [App Store (iOS)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/cofe-general-synod/id1447099638)
- [Google Play (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aimermedia.Synod)

The page's FAQ covers, in this order:

- Device requirements: iOS 17 or later, Android 13 or later.
- Logging in, and resetting a password via the sign-in screen.
- Timetable and agenda: separate timetables for the main session and fringe events, with a date picker.
- Accessing documents, by type: business done, deemed and contingency business, GS Misc papers, GS papers, members' resources, notice papers, order papers (usually 60 to 90 minutes before each sitting), question papers, timetable and agenda, voting results, worship, other documents.
- Viewing documents: download, split-screen and multi-document view, thumbnails, search within a document, zoom, highlights and notes.
- Favouriting agenda items and documents, collected in the Saved section.
- Contact forms: general enquiries, request to speak, and request to ask a question at Question Time.
- Browsing the archive of past Synods back to February 2023.
- Search across agenda items, fringe events and documents.
- Push notifications for last-minute changes, off by default.
- Troubleshooting: force quit and reopen, check for updates, then contact the Synod Support team.
